Indego Bike Share: where the growth comes from

Ride-minutes grew 18.9%. Most of the gain came from new stations, and riders are moving to e-bikes. My call: a small, reversible e-bike pilot.

Key data

Role
Solo, end to end
Data
2.26M trips, 6 quarterly files, 337 stations
Window
Jan.-Sep. 2026 vs. Jan.-Sep. 2025
Tools
Python, Excel, PowerPoint
Best fit
FP&A, strategic finance, business and data analyst

+19.2%Trips1.03M to 1.23M
59%Of the gainFrom stations opened in 2025-26
+34.9%E-bike tripsStandard-bike trips fell 9.1%

The ask

If Philadelphia's bike share were a for-profit business, how fast is it really growing, and what should leaders do next?

What I did

  • Built a Python pipeline that streams 2.26M trip records and checks every row.
  • Compared each quarter with the same quarter last year to remove seasonality.
  • Split growth by station age and bike type to separate expansion from real demand.
  • Flagged bad data instead of hiding it.
  • Wrote a two-slide brief for executives.

What I found

My call

Pilot e-bikes at 3-5 proven stations for 8-12 weeks. Scale only if ride-minutes per bike-day and margin clear the bar.

Without pricing and cost data, no one can prove growth is too fast or too slow. So start small and reversible.

The ask

How do rates, credit costs and consumer behavior flow through a bank's P&L, and where are the warning signs?

Factor research · Team project

Does sales growth predict stock returns?

Not on its own. The fastest growers delivered the worst returns. A four-factor model fixed that.

Key data

Role
Co-author, team of 4
Window
Dec. 1996 - Dec. 2023
Method
Decile sorts, monthly rebalancing, long-short spread
Best fit
Investment research, portfolio analytics, quant
−6.12%Top-growth decileWorst of all ten
+2.63%4th decileThe sweet spot
+6.7%Model long-shortvs. −5.5% for growth alone
53.8%Model hit ratevs. 47.9% for growth alone

The ask

Investors pay up for revenue growth. Does it pay them back?

What I did

  • Profiled the factor first: median growth 8.2%, heavy right skew.
  • Backtested 10 decile portfolios with monthly rebalancing, plus a long-short spread.
  • Tested it across 5 market regimes, from the dot-com bust to the 2022-23 rate hikes.
  • Combined value, quality, momentum and growth into one weighted model.

What I found

Takeaway

Growth pays up to a point, then investors overpay. Weighted 40% value, 30% quality, 20% momentum and 10% growth, returns rise almost steadily from the bottom decile to the top.

Quant research · Summer 2025

A swing strategy built around risk control

Buy oversold turns only when volatility is falling. Exit on volatility-scaled targets. Result: about half the drawdown of a trailing stop.

Key data

Role
Co-author
Authors
Tri Bui, Aella Nguyen
Faculty
Prof. Liang Ding
Data
TSLA daily, Jan. 2020 - Jan. 2025
Best fit
Investment risk, quant and portfolio analytics
12.2%Max drawdownvs. 22.3% with a trailing stop
1.29Sharpe ratiovs. 0.80 with a trailing stop
1.66Profit factorGross profit / gross loss
8,320Parameter setsTested with a drawdown penalty

The ask

Can a simple reversal signal survive one of the most volatile large caps if risk control comes first?

What I did

  • Required three signals before buying: RSI oversold, RSI turning up, volatility falling.
  • Exited with ATR-based take-profit and stop-loss rules, tested against a trailing stop.
  • Searched 8,320 parameter sets, penalizing drawdowns above 25%.
  • Scored each version on Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown and profit factor.

What I found

Takeaway

Risk rules mattered more than the entry signal. Next: a buy-and-hold benchmark and out-of-sample tests.
